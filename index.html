    Before you send

    • The price on this page is an estimate. The shop confirms the final price after looking at your file.
    • Shipping is added once the shop knows the size and weight of the box.
    • Only send models you made or have permission to print.
    • Some models on sharing sites are licensed for personal use only. The shop may ask about the licence or turn the request down.
    • The shop does not print weapon parts and can turn down any request.
    • Printed parts show fine layer lines. Choose the sanded finish for a smoother surface.

      Questions

      How do I pay?

      Through Etsy. Helmets are bought from their Etsy listing. For a custom print, the shop sends you a custom order on Etsy after confirming the price.

      How long does a print take?

      It depends on the size and how busy the printer is. The shop tells you the timing when it confirms your price.

      What files can I send?

      STL files get an instant estimate. 3MF, OBJ and STEP files, or a link to a model page, get a quote by message.

      Can you print a model I found online?

      Usually. Paste the model's link in the Print tab. Some models are licensed for personal use only, so the shop may ask about the licence.

      What about returns?

      Each Etsy listing shows whether that item can be returned or exchanged.
